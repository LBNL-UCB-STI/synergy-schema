# Bring an existing mapping

Start with the real one-year pilot package, preserving existing payloads and
model ownership. The synthetic proof is supporting evidence, not a demand for
a production migration.

## What we ask

One representative payload, its producer context, named owners, and a few
historical incidents the model teams want the exchange to avoid. We do not
invent an incident when none has been supplied.

The named owners remain the decision authorities for native field meanings and
for accepting the cross-model mapping. Synergy translates their existing work
into a typed contract and validates it; that encoding does not transfer
ownership. The [ownership table](../shared-structure/glossary.md#ownership-and-authority)
makes the roles explicit.

## What we do not ask

We do not ask either model to change its runtime, rename native fields, or
approve a schema by implication.

## What you get

A day-zero receipt, a one-year mapping handshake, and a generated one-page
contract-at-a-glance deliverable.

## Timeline

Discovery starts immediately; the encode-and-return target is five business
days after the representative inputs and owner feedback arrive.
